## 1. Security Architecture & Design
Security architecture is the design of the defensive layers of an information system.

### **Physical Security of IT Assets**
Hardware protection is the foundation of information security.
*   **Access Control:** Physical barriers like biometric scanners, smart card readers, and turnstiles to restrict entry to server rooms.
*   **CCTV and Surveillance:** Constant monitoring to deter physical theft and record unauthorized activities.
*   **Hardware Security Issues:** Protecting against physical tampering (e.g., adding hardware keyloggers) and environmental threats (Fire, HVAC failure).
*   **Data Storage & Device Security:** Policies for managing portable storage like USB drives, including port blocking and hardware encryption.

### **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)**
An IDS monitors a network or systems for malicious activity or policy violations.
*   **Types of IDS:**
    1.  **NIDS (Network IDS):** Analyzes traffic across the entire network subnet.
    2.  **HIDS (Host IDS):** Installed on a specific device (like a server) to monitor internal logs and file integrity.
*   **Detection Methodologies:**
    1.  **Signature-based:** Matches patterns against a database of known threats.
    2.  **Anomaly-based:** Establishes a "baseline" of normal traffic and flags any deviation (effective against Zero-day attacks).

## 2. Cyber Law & The Indian IT Act, 2000
Cyber law provides the legal framework to regulate the internet and punish digital crimes.

### **The Indian Information Technology Act, 2000**
This is the primary law in India for e-commerce and cybercrime.
*   **Section 65:** Tampering with computer source documents.
*   **Section 66:** Hacking and computer-related offenses.
*   **Section 66C:** Punishment for Identity Theft (stealing Aadhaar, Passwords).
*   **Section 66D:** Punishment for cheating by personation (Phishing/Fake profiles).
*   **Section 66E:** Violation of privacy (publishing private images without consent).
*   **Section 67:** Publishing obscene or sexually explicit material in electronic form.

### **Legal Issues in E-Business**
Includes the legal validity of electronic contracts, digital signatures, and the liability of intermediaries (ISPs, Social Media platforms).

## 3. Ethical Issues and Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
Ethics in security involves doing what is morally right, even if not strictly required by law.

### **Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)**
1.  **Copyright:** Protects the expression of ideas (Software source code, Documentation).
2.  **Patents:** Protects unique inventions or technical processes (e.g., a specific compression algorithm).
3.  **Software Piracy:** The unauthorized copying or distribution of software.
4.  **Plagiarism:** Stealing someone's digital work or ideas without credit.

### **Domain Name Issues**
*   **Cybersquatting:** Registering a famous brand's domain name to sell it back to them for profit.
*   **Typosquatting:** Registering a domain that is a common misspelling of a popular site (e.g., "gogle.com").

## 4. Ethical Hacking and Data Privacy
### **Data Privacy and Protection**
The ethical obligation to protect Personally Identifiable Information (PII). In India, Section 43A of the IT Act mandates that companies follow "reasonable security practices" to protect sensitive personal data.

### **Issues in Ethical Hacking**
Ethical hacking (White Hat) is legal, but must follow strict guidelines:
*   **Permission:** Explicit written consent before any testing.
*   **Confidentiality:** Maintaining the secrecy of vulnerabilities found.
*   **Integrity:** Ensuring the testing does not damage the system or data.
