# Module 3: Security & Ethical Issues - Extra Detailed

## 1. Information Security Principles (CIA Triad) (10 Marks)
Every security policy is built on these three pillars:

1.  **Confidentiality:** 
    *   Goal: Protect sensitive data from unauthorized access.
    *   Tools: Encryption (AES/RSA), Access Control Lists (ACLs), Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
2.  **Integrity:** 
    *   Goal: Ensure data is not modified during transit or storage.
    *   Tools: Hashing (MD5, SHA), Digital Signatures, Version Control.
3.  **Availability:** 
    *   Goal: Ensure systems are up and running when needed.
    *   Tools: RAID, Off-site Backups, DDoS protection, UPS/Generators.

## 2. Ethical Hacking: Process and Legalities (10 Marks)
Ethical hacking is "Hacking for Good" with the owner's consent.

### **The 5 Phases of Ethical Hacking (PYQ Detail)**
1.  **Reconnaissance (Footprinting):** Collecting information about the target (active or passive).
2.  **Scanning:** Using tools like Nmap to find open ports, live IP addresses, and vulnerabilities.
3.  **Gaining Access:** Exploiting a vulnerability to enter the system (OS level, Application level, or Network level).
4.  **Maintaining Access:** Using Backdoors or Rootkits to stay in the system for further analysis.
5.  **Clearing Tracks:** Removing logs and history to hide the "attack" (important to simulate a real hacker).

### **Legality vs. Hacking:**
*   **Permission:** Ethical hacking requires a "Letter of Authorization."
*   **Intent:** The goal is to improve security, not cause harm.
*   **Compliance:** Must follow the rules of engagement (e.g., don't crash the server).

## 3. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) in Cyber World
*   **Copyright:** Protects the *expression* of ideas (Source code, Graphic design, Music). Violation is called "Piracy."
*   **Trademarks:** Protects *brand identity* (Logos, Domain names). 
    *   *Cyber Squatting:* Registering "google-india.com" to sell it to Google is a trademark violation.
*   **Patents:** Protects *inventions* (New algorithms or hardware designs).
*   **Trade Secrets:** Protects confidential business info (e.g., Google's Search Algorithm).

## 4. Key Offences & Penalties under the IT Act (Must Know for PYQs)
You must mention these sections to get high marks:
*   **Section 66:** Hacking (Dishonest or fraudulent act). Punishment: Up to 3 years or 5 Lakh fine.
*   **Section 66B:** Receiving stolen computer resources.
*   **Section 66C (PYQ):** Identity Theft (Using another's digital signature/password).
*   **Section 66D:** Cheating by personation (e.g., fake social media profile).
*   **Section 66E:** Violation of Privacy (Capturing/publishing private images without consent).
*   **Section 66F:** **Cyber Terrorism.** Punishment: Up to **Life Imprisonment**.
*   **Section 67:** Publishing obscene material.

## 5. Risk Management and Security Policy
*   **Risk:** Probability of a threat exploiting a vulnerability.
*   **Risk Assessment:** Identifying what assets are at risk and the impact of a breach.
*   **Security Policy:** A written document that governs how an organization handles data. 
    *   *Example:* "All employees must change passwords every 30 days."
*   **Incident Response:** The plan for what to do *after* a breach occurs (Containment, Eradication, Recovery).
